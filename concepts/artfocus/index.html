About Artfocus

Real weddings, told with warmth and craft

We're a photo and video studio built around one belief: every wedding deserves to be remembered exactly as it felt — unscripted, emotional, and beautiful. From the quiet moments of a bride's morning to the first dance, we're there to capture it all.

Based in Armenia and trusted by couples across the diaspora, we travel for weddings in the United States and Europe — bringing the same cinematic eye to every celebration, wherever it happens.
