## 2. Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** *"Գեղեցկացնելը մեր մասնագիտությունն է"* — **"Beautifying is our profession."**

**Mission / core message (from homepage):**
> "Our expertise at OXYGEN Aesthetic & Medical Center is devoted to revealing your inner beauty and potential — giving you the means to enhance your natural, beautiful features while ensuring a flawless result."

**Positioning summary:** A licensed, equipment-forward medical aesthetics center that frames cosmetic work as *revealing* natural beauty rather than transforming it. Credibility (licensing, awards, ultrasound diagnostics) and warmth (staff-first testimonials) are the two pillars the brand leans on equally — it reads as clinical *and* personal, not one at the expense of the other.

**Audience:** Segmented content for both women and men ("Կնոջ համար" / "Տղամարդու համար"), general adult clientele seeking cosmetology, hair removal, and diagnostic ultrasound services.

## 3. Voice & Tone

Recurring words across dozens of on-site testimonials cluster around a consistent emotional register:

- **ջերմ** — warm
- **պրոֆեսիոնալ** — professional
- **հոգատար** — caring / attentive
- **բարեհամբույր** — hospitable, friendly
- **բարձրակարգ** — high-caliber / premium

**Tone in practice:**
- Professional and clinical when describing procedures and credentials (licensing, equipment, diagnostics) — precise, no-nonsense.
- Warm and personal in patient-facing copy and testimonials — individual staff members are named and thanked by name repeatedly, suggesting the brand actively encourages a relationship-driven, not transactional, patient experience.
- Confident but not aggressive marketing language — "flawless look," "highlight your natural features," never hard-sell.

**Guidance for future copy:** write like a trusted, friendly specialist, not an ad. Favor concrete credentials (licensed, named specialists, ultrasound-grade diagnostics) paired with reassuring, individual-attention language.

## 4. Visual Identity

### Logo
- Wordmark **"OXYGEN"** in bold black uppercase sans-serif, paired with a small green leaf/sprout icon above the "O."
- Descriptor line beneath in light gray lowercase: *"aesthetic & medical center"*.
- Logo reads clean, medical-grade, and minimal — no ornamentation.

### Color Palette

**Primary accent — sage green** (from the logo leaf and section headings):
| Swatch | Hex | RGB |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | `#89B485` | rgb(137, 180, 133) |

**Neutrals (site backgrounds, text, borders):**
| Role | Hex | RGB |
|---|---|---|
| Page background | `#FFFFFF` | rgb(255, 255, 255) |
| Section background (light gray) | `#F8F8F8` | rgb(248, 248, 248) |
| Section background (alt) | `#F2F2F2` | rgb(242, 242, 242) |
| Body text | `#333333` | rgb(51, 51, 51) |
| Heading / nav text | `#292929` | rgb(41, 41, 41) |
| Secondary text | `#555555` | rgb(85, 85, 85) |
| Muted text | `#777777` | rgb(119, 119, 119) |
| Border / divider | `#CCCCCC` | rgb(204, 204, 204) |

The palette is intentionally restrained: near-white surfaces, charcoal text, and a single sage-green accent used sparingly for CTAs, headings, and the logo mark. This signals cleanliness and clinical trust rather than a decorative beauty-industry look.

### Typography
- **Typeface:** Roboto (web-safe fallback stack: Roboto, Tahoma, Arial, Helvetica, sans-serif)
- Headings use a light-to-regular weight (h1 observed at weight 400), keeping the tone understated rather than shouty.

### Photography & Imagery
- Real clinic interiors (bright, glass-partitioned treatment rooms, soft pendant lighting) — establishes physical trust in the space.
- Close-up, clean beauty/skincare treatment photography (product application, injectable procedures) rather than stock "lifestyle" beauty shots.
- Before/after gallery is a prominent, dedicated site section — visual proof of results is core to the brand's credibility argument.
- Overall styling: soft, well-lit, clinical-but-comfortable — no harsh contrast or saturated color grading.

## 5. Services Architecture

The site organizes offerings into five main categories (also the primary nav):

1. **Medical cosmetology** — injectable procedures: lip volume augmentation, botulinum therapy (Botox), contour plastic/fillers, plasmolifting & plasma-filler, biorevitalization, mesotherapy
2. **Aesthetic cosmetology** — LED therapy, skin care, carbon peeling, dermabrasion, ultrasonic skin cleansing, deep professional facial cleansing, age-specific/young-skin care lines, face/neck/décolletage massage, cryo-massage (liquid nitrogen), chemical peels
3. **Hair removal** (Մազահեռացում)
4. **Ultrasound diagnostics / ՈՒՁՀ** — abdominal cavity, thyroid, prostate, testicles, lymph nodes, soft tissue, vascular doppler-duplex-triplex, head/neck major vessels
5. **Training / Ուսուցում** — professional courses, positioning Oxygen as an educator/authority in the field, not just a service provider

Additional site sections: Blog, video content, before/after gallery, testimonials, careers ("Թափուր աշխատատեղեր"), contact form, privacy policy.
