## 2. Positioning

ARTFOCUS is a destination wedding photo/video studio serving Armenian couples and diaspora clients across the US, Armenia, and Europe. Content centers on real weddings — candid, emotional, cinematic storytelling rather than posed catalog shots. Story highlights are organized per couple (e.g. "S & Gh," "A & A," "V & P"), reinforcing a personal, one-wedding-at-a-time craftsmanship feel rather than a high-volume studio vibe.

**Suggested product/category for design-system generation:** Photography/Videography Studio, Wedding & Events, Luxury Personal Service.

## 3. Voice & Tone

- Romantic, warm, emotionally intimate — not corporate
- First-person, celebratory captions ("We did it again 💍🥂", "the most touching moment ✨🥹")
- Bilingual (Armenian/English) — keep English copy simple and warm so it reads naturally alongside Armenian
- Signature emoji set: 🎥 📸 🎞️ ✨ 💍 🥂 🤍 🫂 ❤️ ♾️
- Bio uses a stylized small-caps/bubble unicode font for emphasis — a decorative wordmark treatment, not a literal font family (translate the *effect* — elevated, editorial, a little playful — into real web typography rather than reusing unicode tricks)

## 4. Target Audience

- Engaged couples planning weddings in Armenia or destination weddings involving Armenian families abroad
- Diaspora clients in the US/Europe seeking a studio that understands both markets
- Referral-driven — couples browsing past clients' highlight reels for trust/social proof before booking

## 5. Visual Identity

### Color Palette (inferred from feed — verify against actual photo exports before finalizing hex values)

Feed imagery is cinematic and warm: golden-hour indoor lighting, skin-tone-forward color grading, soft highlights, deep neutral shadows. Highlight covers are shot in black & white for an editorial, timeless contrast to the warm feed.

| Role | Suggested color | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (warm neutral) | `#C9A66B` champagne gold | Pulled from warm/golden grading across posts |
| Secondary (deep neutral) | `#2B2622` espresso / near-black | Grounding color, echoes B&W highlight covers |
| Background | `#FAF6F0` warm ivory | Soft, editorial, not stark white |
| Accent | `#8C6A56` warm taupe/rose-brown | Skin-tone-adjacent accent, used sparingly |
| Text | `#1F1B18` charcoal | High-contrast body text on ivory |

**Mood:** elegant, warm, romantic, cinematic — avoid anything cold, neon, or tech-startup-coded (no electric blues/purples, no flat corporate palettes).

### Typography Direction

- Headline: an elegant serif or refined script accent (evokes the bio's decorative wordmark) — e.g. a display serif like Cormorant/Playfair paired with a clean sans body
- Body: simple, warm sans-serif for legibility (bilingual copy needs solid Armenian/Latin character support)

### Imagery Style

- Candid, emotional, in-the-moment — not stiff posed studio shots
- Warm/golden color grading; indoor venue and hotel settings feature heavily
- Mix of full-color cinematic stills for the main feed and black & white for editorial/cover moments
- Real couples as hero imagery > stock photography

### Logo / Profile Image

Current profile picture is a candid warm-toned portrait of a bride and groom (not a wordmark/icon logo). There is no standalone logomark visible on the public profile — treat "ARTFOCUS" as a wordmark-first brand until a dedicated logo asset is supplied.

## 6. Content Pillars

1. Real wedding highlights (per-couple storytelling)
2. Behind-the-scenes / "bride morning" style intimate moments
3. Emotional key moments (first look, vows, reception)
4. Studio credibility (repeat bookings — "We did it again")

## 7. Suggested Landing Page Direction

- **Pattern:** Hero-Centric + Social Proof (large cinematic hero video/photo, followed by portfolio grid and testimonials/highlight-style client stories)
- **Style:** Editorial/cinematic minimalism — generous whitespace, warm ivory background, large full-bleed photography, serif display headlines
- **Sections:** Hero (video reel) → About/Positioning → Portfolio (by couple, echoing IG highlights) → Services (photo/video packages) → Testimonials → Contact/Booking (phone + Instagram DM)
- **CTA:** Booking inquiry / WhatsApp or phone contact, prominent Instagram link (primary channel, 62K followers)
- **Avoid:** cold/tech color schemes, stock imagery, overly corporate layouts, AI-purple gradients
